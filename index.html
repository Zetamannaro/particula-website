THE SOLUTION / NANOGLYPH

Small sensor.
Bigger perspective.

One connected platform. From molecular interactions
to a biological fingerprint you can explore.

01 / SENSE

Nanosensor

NanoGlyph turns dynamic molecular interactions into spectral fingerprints. No predefined biomarker required — so you can explore biology before you know exactly what to look for.

Molecular interactions

02 / READ

NIR plate reader

Capture the nanosensor’s near-infrared signal. Its optical, photostable readout can support repeated, time-resolved measurements when sample integrity is preserved.

Optical fingerprints

03 / INTERPRET

AI software

Explore patterns across spectra and time. Investigate multifactorial disease states, distinguish sample groups and compare drug responses within the context of your study.

Biological insight

Three components. One research workflow.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    Introduce your sample

    Bring a relevant biological sample into contact with the nanosensors.

  2. 02

    Let interactions leave a signature

    Molecules interact with the sensor surface, changing the nanosensor’s optical response.

  3. 03

    Illuminate. Read. Repeat.

    Collect near-infrared spectra. Where sample integrity is preserved, repeated readings can reveal how the fingerprint evolves.

  4. 04

    Connect the patterns

    AI-assisted analysis explores spectral and temporal patterns to help compare samples, conditions and responses.

USE CASES

When one marker
isn’t the whole story.

Potential applications for complex biology.
Built to be explored with your research.

01Drug-response screening+

Explore whether a treatment shifts a sample’s molecular fingerprint, and compare responses across candidate compounds or conditions.

02Complex disease biology+

Study biology distributed across multiple molecular changes, especially when no single validated biomarker captures the full picture.

03Patient stratification research+

Investigate whether spectral fingerprints distinguish biologically different sample groups that appear similar by other measures.

04Longitudinal monitoring+

Follow changes over time with repeated optical measurements in assays designed to preserve sample integrity.

05Early phenotypic screening+

Explore a broader molecular readout to help prioritize samples or conditions for deeper downstream characterization.

06Unknown biomarkers+

Start exploring without choosing a protein to measure in advance. Capture a molecular fingerprint when the relevant biomarkers are still unknown.
